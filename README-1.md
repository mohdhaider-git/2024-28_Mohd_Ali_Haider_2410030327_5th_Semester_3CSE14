## 📌 About the Simulation

The Mastercard Cybersecurity Job Simulation is a self-paced virtual work experience that provides practical exposure to the role of a Security Analyst on a Security Awareness team.

The simulation focuses on **phishing awareness**, including designing a phishing simulation and interpreting simulated campaign results to support targeted security-awareness training.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the threat phishing poses to an organisation and its employees.
- Recognise common characteristics and red flags of phishing emails.
- Understand Mastercard's approach to phishing prevention and mitigation.
- Design a phishing email simulation for security-awareness testing.
- Interpret phishing simulation results.
- Recommend targeted security-awareness training based on simulation findings.

## 🗂️ Tasks Completed

### Task 1 — Designing a Phishing Email Simulation

Reviewed a sample phishing email, identified indicators such as a mismatched sender address, generic greeting, urgent language and suspicious links, and revised the email into a more convincing awareness-testing exercise.

### Task 2 — Interpreting Phishing Simulation Results

Reviewed simulated campaign outcome data, identified employees or teams at greater risk, prioritised areas for follow-up training, and developed a targeted security-awareness recommendation.
